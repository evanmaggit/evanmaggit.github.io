About

Horror with atmosphere, bite, and a low budget.

This portfolio highlights my three short films built around dread, identity, family, and the strange emotional logic of fear. Each of my passion projects uses horror as a pressure chamber: small spaces, a Gen-Z lense, and interesting characters pushed toward the thing they least want to face.
